How we engage

MIH Demand-Side Assessment

For fire and EMS departments facing rising call volume and a supply-side strategy that’s running out of runway, the MIH Demand-Side Assessment is a focused 3-week engagement that answers a specific question: does Mobile Integrated Health belong in your operational picture, and if so, at what scale and in what configuration.

From $9,950 3 weeks, start to recommendation For fire and EMS departments, FQHCs, and health systems
What you get

Three weeks. A clear answer.

01

Structured assessment

A 3-week structured assessment of your call profile and demand patterns — who’s calling, why, and what share of volume a demand-side approach could realistically affect.

02

Written report

A 15–25 page written report with a clear recommendation — not a data dump, but a decision document your leadership can act on.

03

Leadership briefing

A 60-minute leadership briefing at the end of the engagement, walking your decision-makers through the findings and the recommendation.

04

90-day follow-up

A 90-day follow-up call as the picture develops — so the recommendation stays useful as conditions change.

What it answers

Four questions, answered with your data.

01

Is MIH the right next investment?

Is MIH the right next investment for your department, or is the supply-side build the right answer?

02

At what scale, in what configuration?

If MIH fits, at what scale and in what configuration — so you invest in the right size of program, not a guess.

03

How much volume is addressable?

What share of current call volume is realistically addressable through demand-side strategy?

04

What does the path cost?

What does the implementation pathway look like, and what does it cost to sustain past initial funding?

Working together

A light lift for your team.
